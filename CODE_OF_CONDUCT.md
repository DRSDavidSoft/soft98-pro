# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Welcome questions and good-faith disagreement.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and outcomes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled with discretion and fairness.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
